## 1. What do the remaining GE7 fields select?

Mostly answered in [microcode.md](../platforms/express/microcode.md#the-data-path) (operand selects 0, 1, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, the register file with REGC and REGD on the ALU and REGA and REGB on the multiplier, REGWR with
REGFDEST = 3, the constants block behind ALUA = 5, the result taps, RAM0DEST = 3 and RAM1DEST = 1 and 3). Still open, with the routes tried:

- **Select 4.** Select 7 is settled (the multiplier output), the ROM selects are settled, and MPYB = 6 is the previous ALU result (inferred, strong) (the pixel-DMA routines, [microcode.md](../platforms/express/microcode.md#the-pixel-dma-routines-as-known-answers)). Eighth pass: the constant-zero reading is refuted for the pixel-DMA routines (inferred, strong): with it the right edge they park in register 15 never reaches RE3 `0x21`. Two readings remain, the previous ALU result and a register held by RESEN; they pass every known answer and differ only at GE7 1456, which no host test checks by value (the stride DMA, whose host test waits for the finish flag only). Open: why RECTI2D's coordinates are doubled under the previous-result reading, and why 185 of 263 routine starts read select 4 before any RESEN word (history-dependent under both remaining readings). Routes tried: the engine on every diagnostic routine under the three readings, RECTI2D, the dependency of each RE write on each operand, the statistic of routine starts, the register-15 round trip of the three DMA routines. Next: a host-checked routine whose first word reads select 4 (none in the diagnostic images), or the GL routines of the full image that add 0.5 or 1.0 (K[0x41], K[0x7c]) through select 5 and select 4 together.
- **MPYA = 4, 6 and 7, MPYB = 7.** No known answer. In the diagnostic image only words 138 and 147 (MPYA = 4) multiply and read the product, in a copy of a console routine that no host test sends. Next: the PROM image's words 100 to 104 and 215 to 234 (FMPY and IMPY with these selects) on a routine with a host-checked output, if one is found (LINE2I is not used by the PROM). Eighth pass, routes tried without a result: the GL core's tokens for the 2D integer primitives (it never sends LINE2I, DRAWCHAR or RECTCOPY: its FIFO offsets are the `+0x42xxx` and `+0x5xxxx` sets, and the X server's own routines are not host-checked either), the Z-plane routines (`gr2_chkzplanes`: no multiply with these selects), the 4.0.5G PROM (the same two console images as 4.0.5D). No shipped host code checks the output of a routine that multiplies with MPYA = 4, 6 or 7 or MPYB = 7.
- **RESEN, REGFDEST 1 and 2, REGE, REGSEL, RAM2DEST, RAM0DEST and RAM1DEST values other than 1 and 3, MISCSRC, DSEL.** No known answer needs them. Routes tried: field statistics (REGFDEST 1 and 2 occur 295 and 395 times in `ge7.bin`
  and the diagnostic image's FpuCondTest tail, words 1911 to 1913, uses 2 with RAM0AD values 0x20 and 0x40). Next: the other diagnostic tests (SpfCondTest's table compare) and the `ide` register-file test sequences.
- **The upper bits of the ROM outputs.** The known answers fix bits 22:16 only. The GL words that use MPYB = 4 and 5 (199 FMPY and 27 MPASSB words) probably take the whole output as a float. Next: a Newton step in the full image (`FMPY` with the seed ROM followed by the correction) run with known operands.

## 2. How is control flow resolved in the GE7 routines?

Mostly answered in microcode.md: conditions 1 and 5 always branch and 2 and 6 never; conditions 3, 4, 7 and 8 branch on a flag line two words behind, the lines `0x62`, `0x63`, `0x64`, `0x68`, `0x69`, `0x6b` are decoded; `0x31`, `0x32` and `0x33` carry a
shared-RAM address (`0x32` does not jump); the stall ends a routine, with the finish-flag evidence. Still open:

- **The senses of conditions 4 and 8 (and the `0x60` sites).** Seventh pass: no second known answer exists in the held programs; the cond 8 routine at GE7 1042 is started by slot 51, which no host function of `ide.IP20` or PROM 4.0.5D sends (only the interactive `gr2_wrFIFO` could). Shown, under the passing-path outcomes, that no line truth value and per-condition polarity fits GeSeqTest's six `0x60` / `0x62` sites of conditions 3, 4 and 8, whose ALU flags are identical at every stage ([microcode.md](../platforms/express/microcode.md#the-data-path)). Either conds 4 and 8 read another flag source or one of the six outcomes
  is wrong. Routes tried: both polarities, stages 0 to 3 on equal, zero, neg, less and greater, the passing-path enumeration with and without stalls ending the routine. Next: a run of slot 51 on a real board or another host program that sends it, a second known answer for cond 4 and cond 8 (GE7 24623 on has nine cond 4 words on `0x69`, no host answer), and a negative case for `0x63`, `0x6b`, `0x68`, `0x69`: the
  diagnostic image has none (every one of the 92 FpuCondTest branches is a positive test, and the other diagnostic users of cond 3, 4, 7 and 8 use `0x60`, `0x62`, `0x64` and `0x77` only); the console routines use `0x61`, `0x62` and `0x64`; in `ge7.bin` `0x63` has 33 sites, `0x6b` 72, `0x68` 2 and `0x69` 83, none with a host answer.
  Eighth pass, routes tried without a result: the GL core's cull and face tokens carry host values (`PassCullFace` sends cull-front to slot 27 and cull-back to slot 28, `GL_FRONT` 1 and 0, `GL_BACK` 0 and 1, both for front and back; with the cull enable bit clear `PassCullFace` sends nothing, and `__glExpEnableCullFace` sends 0 and 0 to the two slots; `PassFrontFace` sends 0 for `GL_CW` and 1 for `GL_CCW` to slot 264 [binary, `libGLcore.so` n32, `__glExpPassCullFace`, `__glExpPassFrontFace`]) but the GE routines they start (GE7 2201, 2216, 2209) only record the word, each with a cond 7 test on `0x62` followed by a cond 5 jump; the routine that tests a polygon's facing against them was not found (the RAM0 slots 32 and 33 read in `ge7.bin` are mostly context copies and X-server routines, not established as that state), and no host code checks a cull outcome. The raster position readback (`__glExpGetRasterPosData`, slot 263, GE7 1374) returns four floats with no branch. A negative case for `0x63`, `0x6b`, `0x68`, `0x69` needs a routine whose outcome the host checks; none was found in the GL core, the PROMs or the diagnostic program. Library: no IRIX 5.3 media or other Express GL binary could be located (the Library's document search does not index installation media; the one other `libGLcore.so` it lists, from a Hot Mix disc, was not read).
- **What `0x60`, `0x61`, `0x77`, `0x6f`, `0x67` and the values above `0x77` select.** `0x60` and `0x61` follow ISUB, IRSUB and FSUB at N-2 and look like compare outcomes (less, greater); `0x6f` follows IPASSA (73 of 144 at N-2); `0x67` follows AND
  (72 of 86). Next: simulate more of the full image with the engine and look for which values make a loop terminate.
- **What conditions 9, 12 and 13 do, and whether cond 11 is the FIFO-drain wait** (13 of 14 sit on the `0x5f` flush write). Cond 9 is not needed by the store counter (CMOV2I has none); in FpuCondTest the cond 9 word is also the IADD
  that bumps the failure count. Next: cond 12 and 13 at GE7 6526 to 6666 with the engine.
- **The control `0x35` words** (63, fields up to `0xf280`) and `0x33` (16, up to `0x7fdf`), and the one-hot `0x34` codes against the three status bits (8 raised the host's finish flag 2; 2 and 4 follow DMA tokens whose host poll
  reads bit 2). Routes tried: statistics of fields and contexts, the host's `diag_finish`, `pix_dma` and DMA tests. Next: HQ2 register decode of the finish flags in [devices/hq2](../devices/hq2/README.md).

## 2a. RE3 command data

Answered for the span and point paths, INIT and RECTI2D: the values are in [devices/re3](../devices/re3/README.md#addresses-the-microcode-writes) and [microcode.md](../platforms/express/microcode.md#recti2d-the-values-it-writes). Open: the meaning of `0x00`, `0x08` and the bits of the INIT words, the roles of `0x5e` and `0x6f`, the data of LINE2I (y1 does reach a write; its slope path to `0x08` needs the ROM's upper bits and the unproven MPYA and MPYB selects) and the operand slots of DRAWCHAR and RECTCOPY (neither has a branch on an unknown line). `0x67` is the direction of a pixel DMA (0x180000 write, 0x100000 read) and `0x20`, `0x21`, `0x24`, `0x25` are the rectangle edges (pixel-DMA routines). Routes tried: GeFifoTest known answer, the console routines run on the engine, INIT in two builds, RECTI2D's dependency of each write on each operand. Next: schematic sheet 12 (vendor-derived, so inferred at best), and the float form of RECTI2D's coordinates against the RE3 floating-point inputs if the TR's iteration description gives them.

## 3. HQ2 ops outside the console set

Partly answered (microcode.md, "HQ2 words beyond the console set"): `0x80` words are status masks that the following type-6 or
type-7 branch tests (`0x0060` is the poll mask); the ops `0x01` to `0x07` with a branch-shaped operand are conditional branches whose
bits 15:13 select one of eight conditions; bits 9:5 of `0x40`/`0x60` are the slot and bits 4:0 a small number that is not part of it. Open: which
`0x80` bit is which, which condition each selector and op number tests, the meaning of bits 4:0 and 15:10 of the read words, the `0x20`
operands other than `0x2000` and `0x0000` (`0x6464` and 16 more), and the effects of `0x10`, `0x22`, `0x81`, `0x90` to `0xd0` (they appear only
in the diagnostic HQ2 image's self-test sequences and in `hq2.bin` as about 400 words). Routes tried: the ShramTest, GeFifoTest and FloatOps
walks, operand-field statistics, the sixteen-word selector sweep. Next: the HQ2 register map (devices/hq2) to name status bits 5 and 6, and the
GL tokens that use the rarer ops (vertex-attribute path in pipeline.md).

## 4. Header words and the second record half-word

The record trailer is a checksum (settled, see microcode.md). Still open from ge7.md item 3: the header words at `0x08` and `0x18`, and

## 5. What is the mechanism behind the separate state namespace?

Settled that the constants block (control `0x3f` reads) and the words that stores and reads name (`0x11` to `0x13`, `0x31` to `0x33` fields) are not one memory at one base: `ge7.bin` has 293 explicit store words naming 126 addresses, 44 of them also read as constants (K[0x41] = 0.5, K[0x8d] = 5 and K[0x3d] among them) ([microcode.md](../platforms/express/microcode.md#the-data-path)). Open: whether the state
words are a second memory or the same shared RAM with a base offset. No host-visible known answer goes through a state address. Routes tried: the loader copy (512 words to shared word 0 in both loaders), every store site against every constants read, the diagnostic image's own `0x67` / `0x68` use, the host side of `gr2_shramre3dma` and `gr2_dma_cpu_ge7` (they address shared word 255 by DMA, not by a state address). Seventh pass: the host side of GE7 1294 is read (`gr2_shramre3dma`, `gr2_stridedma`): the routine stores the shared word 255 (`C[0xee]`) as the start address of the words the host wrote there, next to its count (inferred), and no host test sends slot 51 (GE7 1042). Next: the 1752 routine of the full image and the `0x35` words of the DMA routines (field 1 for a read path, 9 for the write path at GE7 1236 and 1243: inferred from the flag branch, not checked).
